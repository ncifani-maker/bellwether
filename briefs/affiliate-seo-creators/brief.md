# Brief — Affiliate SEO / creator prospecting list · web lane

*Not a brand. A reusable prospecting list: sites that review comparable DTC home and lifestyle brands are
the likeliest to review the next one. Imported 2026-10-06 from
`~/Downloads/Affiliate SEO-Creator Seed List.xlsx` (source untouched). See CHANGELOG D9 for why a list
lives in `briefs/` before any brand does.*

## What it is
159 DTC brands across 19 categories, each searched as "[brand] review" and "[brand] reviews". The output is
a pool of review sites, tagged by the categories they cover, that any consumer brief can draw on.

## Rules (from the source sheet's Instructions tab)
- Page 1 only: one search per query (`--depth 10`).
- Keep results that are a review, a reviews page, a comparison review, or a product-testing article.
- Skip the brand's own site, unless it carries third-party customer reviews worth having.
- Skip Reddit and forums, unless UGC is being tracked deliberately.

## Status of the seeds

  were pulled by hand. Not re-run.
- **approved (64):** "[brand] review" for the 64 brands with no completion date.
- **proposed (64):** "[brand] reviews" for the same 64, held until a 3-brand test shows whether the plural
  surfaces usable sites the singular misses.
