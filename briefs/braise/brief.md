> **Fictional.** Braise does not exist. It's a stand-in for the partner-discovery talk, so a demo never

> Everything downstream of the brand is real: the review sites are real businesses, and the brands they

## What it sells
Non-toxic ceramic-coated cookware, direct to consumer. The hero product is the Braiser, a 4.5-quart
ceramic-coated braiser at $145. There's also a 7-piece set at $395. It's sold only on its own site, with
free shipping and a 100-day trial.

## ICP
Home cooks, 28–45, replacing nonstick pans over PFAS concerns, and people buying for a wedding or
housewarming registry. Demand side only.

## Categories and comparable brands  **for seeds**
- **Categories:** Kitchen / cookware; kitchen tools & appliances (Nicole, 2026-11-04). Not "home": it
  would let vacuums, faucets and power tools in.
- **Comparable brands:** Caraway, Our Place, GreenPan, Great Jones, HexClad. These are product brands,
  and the 2026-10 run found creator blogs for every one of them.
- **Known partner sites (optional):** none. It's a new, fictional brand. Its review sites are the 32
  cookware reviewers from the affiliate-seo-creators run, in `briefs/braise/review-sites.csv`, which
  `/snowball braise` reads without a path. Nicole categorized them by site type and niche. Media sites
  stay in (her call); the competitor's site and the cologne homonym are skipped.

## Conversion event  **Required**
A first order on the brand's site. 30-day cookie, tracked through the affiliate network (fictional:
Impact).

## Asks by motion  **Required**
- **editorial-outreach:** a gifted Braiser for an honest review, or inclusion in an existing
  "non-toxic cookware" roundup or a Caraway / Our Place comparison. No paid guarantee of coverage.
- **referral-partner:** not used in the demo.
- **marketplace:** none. It's sold only on its own site.
- **paid-placement:** not used in the demo.
- **creator-sponsorship** (YouTube lane): not used in the demo.

## Hard exclusions  **Required**
- Retailers and marketplaces (Amazon, Williams Sonoma, Target): they sell cookware, they don't review it.
- Coupon and deal sites.
- Health systems and medical publishers: PFAS articles rank, but they don't take product placements.
- Other cookware brands' own blogs, including "X vs Y" pages a brand publishes about itself.

## Competitor test
Both sides of the transaction. A cookware brand's own comparison page is a competitor, not a partner.
A creator who reviewed Caraway is a partner prospect.
